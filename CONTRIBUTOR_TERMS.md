# Contributor Terms

> Draft. These terms will be finalized before the first contribution is merged.

By opening a pull request or sending work for Ebbtide, you agree to the following.

1. **Your own work.** The contribution is your original work. It contains no AI-generated material and nothing copied from someone else.
2. **Permission to use it.** You give Epheria, the developer of Ebbtide, a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual and irrevocable license to use, edit, reproduce and distribute your contribution in Ebbtide, its demos, updates and promotional material.
3. **You keep ownership.** You still own your work and may use it elsewhere, for example in a portfolio.
4. **Credit.** You are credited in the game under the name you choose. You may also ask to stay anonymous.
5. **No payment.** Contributions are voluntary and unpaid, unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.
6. **Voice recordings.** If you submit voice recordings, you agree that your voice may be edited (for example with radio effects) and used for the character or role you recorded.
7. **Under 18.** If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to these terms with you. Say so in your pull request or issue, and a maintainer will contact you.
